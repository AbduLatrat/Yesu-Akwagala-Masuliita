About Our School

Building a solid foundation for both academic and career growth

Yesu Akwagala Secondary & Vocational School is dedicated to providing high-quality secondary education combined with hands-on vocational skills training. Our goal is to mentor disciplined, innovative, and self-reliant students equipped to excel in university education or direct professional endeavors.

Situated in a calm learning environment in Masuliita Town Council, the school offers modern classroom facilities, dedicated instructors, and practical laboratories.
